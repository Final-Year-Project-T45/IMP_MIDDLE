# FINSECURE ACCOUNT FREEZE & EMERGENCY LOCK POLICY (POL-SEC-004)

1. IMMEDIATE FREEZE CONDITIONS:
- Customer report of lost, stolen, or compromised credentials/cards.
- Detected suspicious burst of failed logins or unauthorized transaction attempts.
- Formal request from Fraud Investigation Unit or Law Enforcement.

2. OPERATIONAL PROCEDURE:
- Account status must immediately transition to 'FROZEN'.
- All outbound transfer requests, debit operations, and card authorizations must be blocked.
- An immediate Audit Event must be logged with the agent/analyst ID and reason.
- Account holder must be notified via SMS/Email within 15 minutes.

3. UNFREEZE PROTOCOL:
- Customer must present multi-factor identity verification in branch or via secure video KYC.
- Fraud Analyst sign-off required prior to transitioning account status back to 'ACTIVE'.
