# FINSECURE LOAN DISBURSEMENT POLICY (POL-LON-005)

1. PRE-DISBURSEMENT CHECKLIST:
- Loan application must be in status 'APPROVED'.
- Customer identity and credit assessment must be fully verified.
- Disbursement amount must match the sanctioned loan amount exactly.
- Account status of beneficiary must be 'ACTIVE'.

2. DISBURSEMENT EXECUTION:
- Credit customer account with sanctioned amount.
- Update loan disbursement status to 'DISBURSED' with timestamp.
- Record disbursement transaction with reference code 'LN-DISB'.
- Disbursement is ONE-TIME and IDEMPOTENT. Repeated disbursement attempts on an already disbursed loan must be rejected.
