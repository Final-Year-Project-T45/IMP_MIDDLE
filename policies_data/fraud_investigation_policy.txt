# FINSECURE FRAUD INVESTIGATION POLICY (POL-FRD-006)

1. FRAUD CASE HANDLING:
- Fraud cases (e.g. FC-2291) track suspect activity, compromised credentials, or high-risk transfers.
- Analysts and agents can query case status, severity level (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL), and linked transactions.
- Status workflow: OPEN -> UNDER_INVESTIGATION -> ACTION_TAKEN / RESOLVED.

2. EVIDENCE RETRIEVAL:
- Agents must retrieve associated customer profile, transaction history (last 5-10 transactions), and account freeze records when reviewing a fraud case.
