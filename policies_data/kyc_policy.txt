# FINSECURE CUSTOMER KYC & RISK CLASSIFICATION POLICY (POL-KYC-003)

1. KYC TIER REQUIREMENTS:
- Tier 1: Verified (Full Aadhaar/PAN validation).
- Tier 2: Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-net-worth or corporate accounts.

2. RISK RATING:
- LOW: Standard retail customer with clean transaction history.
- MEDIUM: Accounts with occasional high volume.
- HIGH: Accounts under active fraud monitoring or with recent security alerts.
