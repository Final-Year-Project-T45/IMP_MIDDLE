# FINSECURE RETAIL WIRE TRANSFER POLICY (POL-RET-001)

1. DAILY TRANSFER LIMITS:
- Standard Retail Accounts: Maximum transfer limit per calendar day is ₹2,00,000.
- Single Transaction Limit: Single online wire transfer / IMPS / NEFT limit is ₹1,00,000 without additional 2FA / Manager approval.
- High-Value Transfers (₹80,000 - ₹2,00,000): Allowed automatically if account balance is sufficient and account status is ACTIVE.

2. VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Sender account must be in ACTIVE status.
- Sender must have sufficient clear balance.
- Beneficiary (receiver) account must be verified and ACTIVE.
- Transactions above ₹50,000 must generate an automated audit log entry.

3. RESTRICTIONS:
- Transfers from FROZEN or SUSPENDED accounts are strictly prohibited.
- Transfers exceeding daily limits will be queued for manual review.
