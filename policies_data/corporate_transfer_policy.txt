# FINSECURE CORPORATE WIRE TRANSFER POLICY (POL-CORP-002)

1. DAILY TRANSFER LIMITS:
- Corporate Accounts: Maximum transfer limit per calendar day is ₹50,00,000.
- Dual Authorization required for single transactions exceeding ₹10,00,000.

2. COMPLIANCE & AUDIT:
- All corporate transfers must record tax identification and corporate clearance flags.
